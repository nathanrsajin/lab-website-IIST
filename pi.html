About Prof. Deepak Mishra

Prof. Deepak Mishra joined the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology (IIST) in 2010. He currently serves as a Professor and the Head of the Department of Avionics. He is the founder and Principal Investigator of the CVVR Lab.

His research is dedicated to building robust computer vision systems, image and signal processing models, and deep learning architectures, with applications in satellite pansharpening, remote sensing classification, biometric security, and virtual reality training environments.

Prior to joining IIST, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Louisville Health Science Center, USA, working on biomedical visual modeling. He earned his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IITK) in 2007.

About The Lab

Perception systems for Earth observation and immersive computing.

The CVVR Lab at IIST supports advanced research at the intersection of computer vision, deep neural network modeling, virtual environments, and avionics applications. Our primary mission is to solve high-stakes visual inference challenges relevant to the space sector, disaster management, and medical analytics.

Led by Prof. Deepak Mishra, the group combines robust mathematical formulation with state-of-the-art computational infrastructure. The laboratory is equipped with thermal sensing systems, stereo cameras, high-throughput GigE interfaces, immersive VR setups, and dedicated GPU computing nodes.

We actively collaborate with government research institutions (including ISRO centers and NRSC), top-tier academic institutes, and industrial partners to translate deep learning algorithms into deployable technology for high-resolution optical analysis, landslide monitoring, and training simulation environments.
